Admissions

Information regarding admissions to Narasaraopeta Engineering College for the academic year 2025-2026...

Eligibility Criteria

  • Pass 10+2 with the minimum of 45% aggregate with Physics and Mathematics as mandatory subjects 70% admissions done on the basis of AP EAPCET ranks via online counselling 30% admissions done under “B-Category” by management, based on marks in qualifying examination (10+2 or equivalent), as per the guidelines of the ANDHRA PRADESH STATE COUNCIL OF HIGHER EDUCATION – APSCHE

Application Process

  1. obtain the application form and prospectus, either from the college campus or the official website
  2. Fill in all the necessary details on the application form accurately and completely. Attach all the required documents along with the completed application form.
